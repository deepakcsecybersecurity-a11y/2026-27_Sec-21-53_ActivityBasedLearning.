##### 7\. Advanced Numeric Pattern Generator (PDF Task 27)

START

Input diamond size n.

If n is less than 1 or greater than 9, display an error.

Otherwise, print the upper half:

For i from 1 to n, print n − i spaces.

Print numbers from 1 to i, then from i − 1 down to 1.

Print the lower half:

For i from n − 1 down to 1, print n − i spaces.

Print numbers from 1 to i, then from i − 1 down to 1.

STOP
