##### 10\. Tuple-Based Product Catalogue Audit (PDF Task 49)

START

Initialize the product tuples and input the minimum stock level.

Set total\_value = 0.

For each product tuple:

Unpack product name, price and quantity.

Calculate value = price × quantity and add it to total\_value.

Display product details and stock value.

If quantity = 0, display Out of stock.

Else if quantity < minimum\_stock, display Replenishment needed.

Otherwise, display Stock sufficient.

Display total inventory value.

STOP
